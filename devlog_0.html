THE PLAN OF THIS DEMO

This is not a full test version of the mod, but rather a demo build designed to ensure there are no crashes during this early stage of development, helping us narrow down the potential causes of any issues.
In this release, you will find:

  • A revamped map
  • New factions
  • New (standard) units for each cultural group
  • New buildings (need a lot of work tho)

The primary goal is to test as many scenarios as possible to uncover bugs, errors, and crashes, as well as to gather your feedback and suggestions. Everything included in this build is non-final and subject to change before the official release, depending largely on your input.

For instance, resources have not yet been placed on the map: the initial plan was to restrict resources based on historical presence, but this might severely imbalance gameplay—leaving some regions completely resource-starved and forcing players to rely entirely on trade.
We’d love to hear your thoughts on this: would you prefer strict historical accuracy, or a light vs. heavy gameplay rebalance?

Buildings are also currently very easy to construct (100 denarii and 1 turn), and their effects still need fine-tuning. The main goal here is to determine which buildings add real value and which could be removed. A few might still be missing to add strategic depth, and 3 building slots (+1 for the port) may not be enough—your feedback on this will be invaluable.

Below is the (nearly) complete changelog.

SCRITPS
  • Started to add a provincial population system
    A more dynamic, complex and strategic campaign, in which the player must take into account the composition of their social fabric when managing their territories: the more nobles there are, the greater the wealth, but the smaller the recruitment pool and the greater the opposition from representatives of other cultures; the more tribesmen there are, the larger the recruitable population, provided they are of the same culture or one that is tolerated. Differences between cultures will lead to the need to manage conflicts, rebellions and unrest; you will need to decide whether other cultures should assimilate you into their own culture, show varying degrees of tolerance, or whether you should be forcibly eradicated.
    Actually only the interface has been created.
