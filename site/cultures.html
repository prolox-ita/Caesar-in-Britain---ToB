Cultures of Britannia

Six peoples · Six faiths · Six ways to rule

Romans
1 PLAYABLE
cib_religion_roman

By 55 BCE the Roman Republic has conquered half the known world. Caesar's legions represent the most professional and disciplined military machine of the ancient world — standardized equipment, coordinated tactics, and an institutional memory of centuries of warfare. Roman culture in this mod emphasizes legitimacy, law, and the economic power of empire.

Culture Trait — Pax Romana
Romanitas
+Legitimacy when holding Roman titles; bonus to stability in administered provinces
Ius Civile
+Public order in settled provinces; reduces unrest from recent conquests
Trade Network
+Trade income bonus; Roman roads and infrastructure multiply economic returns

Britanni
12 PLAYABLE · 8 AI
cib_religion_britanni

The native Iron Age tribes of Britain — settled farmers, skilled metalworkers, and fierce warriors who have never bowed to outside rule. The Britanni are defined by their attachment to their land and their sacred groves. Their Druids hold enormous social power, acting as judges, teachers and priests across tribal boundaries. Militarily they rely on chariots, light cavalry, and dense hillfort networks to absorb and bleed invaders.

Culture Trait — Defenders of Britannia
Cradle of Druids
+Religious unity and Druidic authority; bonus to morale when defending sacred territory
Hillforts
+Garrison strength and defensive bonuses for existing fortifications; cheaper hillfort construction

Belgi
2 PLAYABLE · 3 AI
cib_religion_belgi

The Belgic tribes occupy both sides of the channel — coastal Gaul and southeastern Britain — maintaining cultural ties that Rome has now severed. They are the most sophisticated of the non-Roman cultures, with a long tradition of coinage, agricultural surplus, and trade with Mediterranean merchants. Caesar used the Atrebates king Commius as an envoy, making them an ambiguous faction: neither fully Roman allies nor fully independent.

Culture Trait — Continental Heritage
Cultural Isolation
Diplomatic penalty with purely British and purely Gaulish factions; caught between worlds
Advanced Agriculture
+Food surplus from farming; agricultural buildings produce more in Belgic-settled provinces
Warriors Society
+Melee attack for core infantry; warrior aristocracy provides higher-quality base troops

Galli
4 AI ONLY
cib_religion_galli

The Gaulish tribes of coastal Normandy and Brittany — Caletes, Lexovii, Veliocasses, Venellii — are the shoreside backdrop to Caesar's British campaigns. They have all been subjugated or intimidated into submission by this point, but they remain proud and Celtic to the core. Their Druidic networks span the channel, and their cavalry traditions are second to none. All four are AI-controlled in the current build.

Culture Trait — Celtic Fervor

+Charge bonus for all cavalry and elite infantry units on first contact
Aristocratic Cavalry
+Melee attack and morale for noble cavalry units; Gaulish horse are among the finest in the world
Druidic Network
+Diplomacy with other Galli factions; shared sacred sites create strong intra-cultural bonds
Rivalry
Passive tension penalty with neighbouring Galli factions; inter-tribal competition is structural

Hiberni
4 PLAYABLE · 11 AI
cib_religion_hiberni

The peoples of ancient Ireland are beyond the edge of the Roman world. No Roman army has ever set foot in Ireland, and most Romans think it barely exists. The Hiberni are cattle lords and sea raiders — a pastoral people whose wealth is measured in cattle and whose status depends on the generosity of their hospitality. Their warriors are fierce light infantry, mobile and unpredictable, more comfortable in coastal raids than pitched battles.

Sacred Hospitality

Picti
6 PLAYABLE · 5 AI
cib_religion_picti

The painted people of the northern highlands are the most alien culture in the mod — mysterious even to their British neighbours, unknown entirely to Rome. The Picti live in a world of rugged mountains, dark lochs, and dense forest. Their social structure is matrilineal, their warriors fight light and fast, and their knowledge of the land gives them enormous tactical advantage on home terrain. They are the hardest faction group to play — isolated, poorly-equipped compared to Rome, but with deep bonuses on their native ground.

Culture Trait — Sons of the Highlands
Wild Lands
Attrition resistance when operating in highland and forested terrain; Pictish armies do not tire in their own wilderness
Light Steps
+Ambush chance and stealth movement in highland regions; Pictish scouts are nearly invisible
Highland Skirmishers
+Range and reload speed for all skirmisher units; Pictish javelinmen and archers are exceptional on broken ground
