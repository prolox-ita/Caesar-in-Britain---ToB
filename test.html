Caesar's First Expedition

Julius Caesar crosses the channel with two legions — the first Roman commander to set foot on the shores of Britannia. The landing is contested, the terrain unfamiliar, and the tribes resistant. A beachhead is established and then abandoned. Caesar returns to Gaul, but the island's call remains.
