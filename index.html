Welcome to the Medieval Kingdoms 1212 AD
Official Website

After over 5 years in development, Medieval Kingdoms 1212 AD brings the glory of the High Middle Ages to Total War: Attila. Experience authentic medieval warfare with 57 playable factions, over 4,000 period-accurate units, and innovative scripted mechanics.

🗺️

Renewed Campaign Map

Explore a completely revamped medieval world with new settlements, updated regions, and historically accurate geography across Europe and beyond.

⚔️

57 Playable Factions

From the Holy Roman Empire to the Sultanates of the East, command kingdoms across Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa.

🛡️

Over 4,000 Units

Beautifully modeled period-accurate units spanning three centuries of medieval warfare with a three-tier upgrade system.

👑

Scripted Mechanics

Experience crusades, Holy Roman Empire elections, papal politics, population management, and dynamic world events.

Campaign Features

Complete Overhaul

Custom building chains, single-region provinces with 6 building slots each, and completely redesigned gameplay mechanics.

Crusades System

Journey to the Holy Land in service of God, glory, or conquest. Participate in scripted crusade events that reshape the medieval world.

The Papacy

Catholic factions must navigate their relationship with the Pope - earn boons for piety or face excommunication and interdict.

Dynamic Ranks

Any faction can rise from a humble county to a mighty empire through conquest and diplomacy.
