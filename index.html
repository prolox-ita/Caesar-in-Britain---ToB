History Unwritten

Caesars in Britain is a full overhaul of Total War Saga: Thrones of Britannia, set during three crucial periods of Rome's occupation of the British Isles. The mod aims to deliver three distinct campaigns and an entirely new multiplayer experience.

Built by historical enthusiasts who felt abandoned by the official developers, CIB carries a simple spirit: "If they won't give us something to play, why don't we do it ourselves?" It is a community mod — made by the fanbase, for the fanbase.

The legions landed not as conquerors alone, but as players in a drama already millennia in the making. The tribes of Britannia had their own kingdoms, their own gods, and their own reasons to resist.

— Caesars in Britain, mod description

The Roman Invasion

Campaign Overview

The campaign places you at the heart of Rome's most ambitious northern expedition. Following the model of the AD 43 invasion under general Aulus Plautius, you choose where to land your first two legions on the island and establish an initial foothold.

Every decision carries weight. The Britannic tribes are not passive victims — they will unite, divide, negotiate, and betray depending on your movements. Rome's grip on the island is far from guaranteed.

Playing as Rome means managing an ever-shifting coalition of legions, each with its own specialization, while the tribes exploit any vulnerability left exposed on the front lines.

  • 🌎
    Strategic Landing System Choose where your legions make landfall — each position opens different strategic corridors into the island's interior.
  • ⚔
    Roman vs. Britannic Asymmetry Two radically different play experiences — Rome's disciplined might versus the guerrilla resilience of the tribes.
  • 🗹
    16+ Factions Planned From Roman legions to coastal tribes and Welsh kingdoms, each with distinct unit rosters and campaign traits.
  • 🏃
    Custom Assets Models from Rome 2 HD, Imperial Legions units by Leonardo, and assets from the Sol Invictus mod community.

The Legions of Caesar

Legion System

VII

Legio VII

Masters of close-quarters formation warfare. Legio VII fields the finest heavy spearmen available to Rome, capable of holding ground against any Britannic charge. Their weakness lies on the flanks — cavalry screens are nearly nonexistent.

Heavy Infantry Spear Specialists Weak Cavalry

IX

Legio IX Hispana

The Ninth fields a diverse and powerful cavalry arm — the perfect counterpart to the Seventh's infantry-heavy doctrine. In tandem, the two legions form a self-supporting force capable of dominating open terrain and breaking encirclements.

Diverse Cavalry Mobile Warfare Flanking Expert

Innovative Battle Modes

Multiplayer Experience
I

Breakthrough

Legions begin from random positions across the map and must navigate hostile, challenging terrain to reach the safety of their Fort — all within a brutal one-hour window. Failure means facing the Emperor's wrath.

II

Conquest

Villages dot the battlefield, each defended by Britannic tribesmen. Roman legions must storm and hold as many settlements as possible while the Britons contest every inch of ground.

III

Hold the Fort

One Roman player commands a small garrison against overwhelming barbarian waves — and must hold long enough for a relief force to arrive. Cooperation and timing are everything.

IV

Supply Line

Seize encampments and control points to strangle the enemy's logistics. Rome's legions can march forever; a starved tribe cannot fight. Command the land and command the war.
