The Setting

Caesars in Britain is a full overhaul of Total War Saga: Thrones of Britannia, set during the crucial periods of Rome's military presence in the British Isles. The mod replaces the original campaign entirely, delivering multiple distinct historical scenarios — each with its own factions, settlements, rosters, and strategic context.

From Julius Caesar's first tentative landings on the southern shores to the deeper transformations wrought by later invasions, every campaign reflects the evolving face of Roman and Britannic history — changing landscapes, shifting alliances, and peoples caught between two worlds on the edge of the known.
