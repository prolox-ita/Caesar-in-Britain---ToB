The Setting

Caesars in Britain is a full overhaul of Total War Saga: Thrones of Britannia, set during crucial periods of Rome's presence in the British Isles. The mod delivers multiple distinct campaigns spanning different eras, each with its own factions, settlements, and historical context.

From Julius Caesar's first tentative landings on the southern shores to the deeper transformations of the island's peoples and powers, every campaign reflects the evolving face of Roman and Britannic history — changing landscapes, changing alliances, changing worlds.

Caesar's First Expedition

Julius Caesar crosses the channel with two legions — the first Roman commander to set foot on the shores of Britannia. The landing is contested, the terrain unfamiliar, and the tribes resistant. A beachhead is established and then abandoned. Caesar returns to Gaul, but the island's call remains.

The Legions of Caesar

Legion System

Legio VII

Legio VII

Masters of close-quarters formation warfare. Legio VII fields the finest heavy infantry available to Rome, capable of holding ground against any Britannic charge. Their weakness lies on the flanks — cavalry screens are nearly nonexistent.

Heavy Infantry Spear Specialists Weak Cavalry

Legio IX Hispana

Legio IX Hispana

The Ninth fields a diverse and powerful cavalry arm — the perfect counterpart to the Seventh's infantry-heavy doctrine. In tandem, the two legions form a self-supporting force capable of dominating open terrain and breaking encirclements.

Diverse Cavalry Mobile Warfare Flanking Expert
