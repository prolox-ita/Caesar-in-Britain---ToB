EFFECTS & TRAITS

Passive bonuses, modifiers, and faction abilities

1. Culture Traits
Permanent bonuses granted to all factions of a culture group. Active in all dev builds from v0.0.7 onward.
ROMANS — PAX ROMANA
Culture Trait
Romanitas
Increases legitimacy for the Roman faction leader. The weight of Rome's civilization demands order.
Culture Trait
Ius Civile
Roman law brings order and stability to newly captured provinces. +Public Order in governed regions.
Culture Trait
Trade Network
Roman road infrastructure multiplies trade income across all ports. +% Income from Trade.
Culture Trait
Legionary Discipline
Professional legionaries train harder than any tribal warrior. +% Attack, Defense and Morale for all Roman units.
Culture Trait
Mos Maiorum
The old ways bind Roman officers to their commander. +Loyalty for all characters in the faction.
Culture Trait
Roads Builder
Roman legions march on roads they build themselves. +% Campaign Movement Range for all Roman armies.
BRITANNI — DEFENDERS OF BRITANNIA
Culture Trait
Cradle of Druids
Born in Britain, druidic lore binds the tribes under one shared tradition. +Public Order and Allegiance across all Britanni provinces.
Culture Trait
Hillforts
Atop every ridge and hill, the tribes raised earthen ramparts and timber gates. Those who defend them fight with the earth itself at their back. Increases garrison size and settlement defense.
BELGI — CONTINENTAL HERITAGE
Culture Trait
Cultural Isolation
+% Morale and Defense when defending in own territories. The Belgae are strangers in both Britain and Gaul — they fight hardest at home.
Culture Trait
Advanced Agriculture
The Belgae introduced tools like the iron plowshare to northern Europe. Agricultural buildings grant +% Food Production.
Culture Trait
Warriors Society
Belgic warriors are defined by martial excellence above all. +% Melee Attack for all infantry units.
GALLI — CELTIC FERVOR
Culture Trait
Celtic Fervor
The Gauls hurl themselves into battle with terrifying fury, but that fire burns brightest in the first clash. +Charge bonus and Resistance for all Land Units.
Culture Trait
Aristocratic Cavalry
Gallic nobles ride to war on their own horses and at their own expense, making mounted warriors cheaper to raise. Reduced recruitment cost for cavalry units.
Culture Trait
Aristocratic Cavalry (Morale)
Gallic nobles ride to battle with their honour at stake, and would rather die than be seen to flee. +Morale for Cavalry.
Culture Trait
Druidic Network (Diplomacy)
The druids move freely between tribes, untouched by war and feud. Through their counsel, chieftains who share the old faith are more willing to listen, trade and make peace.
Culture Trait
Druidic Network (Loyalty)
Where the druids hold sway, the people look to the sacred groves before their rulers. Loyalty runs deeper in lands that keep the old rites.
Culture Trait
Rivalry Between Tribes
Old feuds and rival clienteles keep the tribes of Gaul forever divided against one another. -% Diplomatic Relations with other Gaulish factions.
PICTI — SONS OF THE HIGHLANDS
Culture Trait
Wild Lands (Attrition)
Born among frozen glens and mountain snows, these peoples endure winters that would break any foreign army. Reduced Winter Attrition for Pictish armies.
Culture Trait
Wild Lands (Movement)
Every hidden path and mountain pass is known to them, and they cross their own lands faster than any stranger. +% Campaign Movement Range in highland terrain.
Culture Trait
Light Steps
Lightly armed and used to rough ground, their warriors strike and withdraw before the enemy can close. +% Speed for Light Troops in battle.
Culture Trait
Highland Skirmishers
From the heights, slingers and javelin-men rain missiles on the enemy long before the lines meet. +% Ammunition for Missile Infantry.
HIBERNI — CHILDREN OF THE WESTERN SEA
Culture Trait
Raiders
Raiding is the path to glory; cattle, slaves and treasure taken from enemies fill the halls of kings. +% Income from sacking and raiding enemy settlements.
Culture Trait
Cattle Wealth
Wealth is measured in cattle, and great herds feed the people and the warband alike. +% Food Production from cattle farming buildings.
Culture Trait
Sacred Hospitality
Guests are sacred and a lord's open table binds his people to him, keeping peace throughout the land. +Public Order in all provinces.
Culture Trait
Isolated
Beyond the sea, the Hiberni lie far from the trade routes that enrich Britannia and Gaul. -% Trade income from ports and markets.

3. Battle Context Bonuses
These bonuses activate during battle based on context — defending your own territory, unit type, or relationship to the enemy.
Battle
Territory Defense Bonus
Morale and melee defense bonus when fighting inside your own territory. The home-ground advantage is real.
Battle
Enemy Attrition (Own Territory)
Enemies suffer additional attrition when moving through your lands. Raiding armies wear down in hostile ground.
Battle
Legionary Discipline
Roman units gain attack, defense, and morale bonuses tied to unit discipline. The legion is not merely trained — it is a machine.
Battle
Chariot Bonus (Britanni)
Britanni chariots have modified recruitment cost and special experience bonuses. Caesar himself was impressed by their battlefield effectiveness.
Battle
Cavalry Speed
Cavalry movement speed modifier tied to culture trait. Gallic and Roman horses move faster than the ponies of the north.
Battle
Unit Fatigue Resistance
Some northern cultures have reduced fatigue effects. Pictish and Hiberni warriors are hardened by terrain that would exhaust a Roman legionary.
4. Diplomatic & Subculture Modifiers
Passive relations bonuses between factions of the same cultural group, and special subculture diplomacy from the Druidic Network.
Diplomacy WIP
Gallic Culture Diplomacy
Galli factions receive positive diplomatic modifiers with other Galli tribes. A common tongue and shared gods ease negotiation.
Diplomacy WIP
Belgic Culture Diplomacy
Belgi factions receive positive diplomatic modifiers with other Belgi tribes. Continental kinship runs deep even under Roman pressure.
Diplomacy WIP
British Culture Diplomacy
Britanni factions receive positive diplomatic modifiers with other Britanni. Shared language and custom create a foundation for alliance.
Diplomacy WIP
Hibernian Culture Diplomacy
Hiberni factions receive positive diplomatic modifiers with other Hiberni. Shared Brehon law and sacred hospitality bind the Irish clans.
Diplomacy WIP
Pictish Culture Diplomacy
Picti factions receive positive diplomatic modifiers with other Picti. The painted people answer to their own councils, not Rome's.
Diplomacy WIP
Druidic Network
Extra diplomacy bonus specifically for Galli and Britanni factions sharing the druidic tradition. The druids serve as neutral go-betweens across tribal lines.
